Health Tips

Maintain a Healthy Lifestyle

Simple daily habits can greatly improve your overall health and prevent many diseases.

  1. Eat balanced meals with fruits and vegetables.
  2. Exercise for at least 30 minutes daily.
  3. Drink plenty of clean water.
  4. Get enough sleep every night.
  5. Avoid excessive alcohol and smoking.

Prevent Common Infections

Practicing good hygiene helps protect you and others from illness.

  1. Wash your hands regularly with soap.
  2. Keep your environment clean and ventilated.
  3. Cover your mouth when coughing or sneezing.
  4. Avoid sharing personal items.
  5. Visit the clinic when symptoms persist.

Heart Health Care

Taking care of your heart reduces the risk of serious health conditions.

  1. Reduce salt and fatty food intake.
  2. Monitor your blood pressure regularly.
  3. Maintain a healthy body weight.
  4. Manage stress through relaxation techniques.
  5. Schedule regular medical checkups.
